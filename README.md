## Summer Pockets
> "A summer that will never be forgotten."

## Short Description
Summer Pockets is a story follows a young man named Hairi Takahara who visits a peaceful island called Torishirojima during his summer vacation. As he meets new people, he begins to discover the island's mysteries and experiences a summer filled with friendship, memories, and emotions.

## Key Information
* **Genre:** Drama, Mystery, Romance, Slice of Life
* **Based on:** Visual Novel by KEY
* **Studio:** Feel
* **Episodes:** 20

## Main Characters
1. **Hairi Takahara** - The main character who visits Torishirojima during summer vacation.
2. **Shiroha Naruse** -  A quiet girl who seems to have a connection to the island's mysteries.
3. **Ao Sorakado** - A cheerful girl interested in the island's legends.
4. **Kamome Kushima** - A girl searching for a mysterious treasure.
5. **Tsumugi Wenders** - A girl who spends her time near the island's lighthouse.

## Supporting Characters
1. **Umi Katou** - Hairi's young relative who comes to the island during summer vacation.
2. **Kyouko Misaki** - Hairi's aunt who lives on Torishirojima.
3. **Miki Nomura** - A girl who belongs to the island's youth group and helps maintain order.
4. **Shizuku Mizuori** - The student council president who visits the island during summer.
5. **Tenzen Kanou** - A boy who is very passionate about table tennis.
6. **Ryouichi Mitani** - A boy from the island who is known for his unusual behavior.
